# VerdaFresh

> VerdaFresh develops water-based, saran-free oxygen barrier coatings for recyclable
> flexible food packaging. Its coatings are applied to BOPP, HD-BOPE, MDO-PE and PET
> film substrates to extend food shelf life while keeping the finished package
> mono-material and recyclable. VerdaFresh is a physical-products manufacturer based
> in Wayne, Pennsylvania — it publishes no API, SDK, developer portal or other
> machine-readable interface.

## No API surface

VerdaFresh has no developer program. As of 2026-09-02 there is no public API, no
OpenAPI or other machine-readable contract, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no
SDK on any public package registry, and no GitHub organization. The subdomains
api., developer., developers., docs. and app.verdafresh.com do not resolve.
Do not attempt to call a VerdaFresh API; none exists.
